Privacy Policy · Version 2026-11

Your cycle is your business

Effective 12 November 2026. This policy explains what Bloom does with your information, in plain language.

The short version

  • Everything you log stays on your phone, encrypted, unless you choose to turn on cloud backup.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Cloud backup is off until you create an account and agree to it. You can turn it off and delete it at any time.
  • Reminders are created on your phone and are discreet by default.
  • Bloom is not a method of contraception and does not diagnose anything.

1. Who we are

Bloom is published by Accra Resource Center LBG (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, email richsarbah11@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Bloom works fully without an account. What you enter is stored only on your device, in an encrypted file (AES-256). The key that unlocks it is kept in your phone’s secure keychain and is marked so that it never leaves that device — not even in your phone’s own backups.

This includes:

  • Period dates and flow.
  • Daily check-ins: symptoms, moods, energy, cramp strength and what helped, sleep, water, activity, and — if you add them — weight and temperature.
  • Medications and pill-pack details, and which ones you ticked.
  • Private journal entries and notes.
  • Your settings, an optional first name, an optional year of birth, and your goals.

We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app, or using Profile → Delete all my data, removes it from your phone.

Features that use your phone, not our servers

  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. By default the lock screen shows only “A gentle reminder from Bloom” — no mention of periods, fertility or medicines unless you switch discreet notifications off.
  • App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your passcode through your phone’s own system. Bloom never receives your biometric data.
  • The home screen widget shows only cycle numbers (for example “Day 14 · 14 days to go”). It shows period wording only if you choose the detailed widget. Its numbers are shared between Bloom and the widget on your phone, nowhere else.
  • Predictions, patterns and insights are calculated on your phone.

3. Optional cloud backup

If you create an account and turn on cloud backup, Bloom copies your records to our server so you can restore them on a new phone or keep two phones in step. It stays off until you choose it, and we record when you agreed or withdrew.

Each person’s records are locked to their own account by the database itself (row-level security), so one account can never read another’s data.

4. When you share something

Some features create a file only when you ask, and hand it to your phone’s share sheet. You decide where it goes; Bloom does not upload it or keep a copy.

  • Doctor visit report — a PDF of your cycles, symptoms and medications. Your name and notes are left out unless you include them.
  • Export my data — a backup file of everything you’ve logged. Anyone with this file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Links to articles (such as the NHS or ACOG) open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

5. What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Show ads, or use advertising identifiers or tracking across apps and websites.
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting tools that collect your usage.
  • Use your health data to train AI. Bloom has no AI features today; if we add one, it will ask before reading anything.
  • Share your data with employers, insurers or anyone else — except where the law requires it (see below).

6. Why we may use your data

Information about your periods, symptoms and health is sensitive. We only process it on our server with your explicit consent, which you give when you turn on cloud backup and can withdraw at any time. Your email address and sign-in records are used to provide the account you asked for and to keep it secure.

If we receive a legally binding request from a court or authority, we will only disclose what the law strictly requires, will challenge requests we believe are unlawful, and will tell you unless the law forbids it. Because data that never leaves your phone is not on our server, we cannot hand it over.

7. Where data is stored

Cloud backup runs on servers we operate on Railway (railway.com), in the United States (US West). Railway hosts the servers; it does not use your data for its own purposes. If you live outside the United States, your backed-up data is transferred there; we rely on your explicit consent and on the safeguards in this policy for that transfer.

8. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it or the app.
  • Cloud backup: until you delete it. Deleting your account removes your records from our database straight away.
  • Server backups: we take an encrypted backup of the database every night and keep each one for 30 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.
  • Consent history: kept with your account and deleted with it.

9. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and take your data — Profile → Export my data, or ask us for a copy of what’s on our server.
  • Correct it — edit any entry in the app.
  • Delete it — Profile → Delete all my data erases your phone and, if you’re signed in, your cloud account. Or email us.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off cloud backup at any time. This doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew.
  • Complain — to us first if you like, or to your data protection authority (for example, Ghana’s Data Protection Commission, or the authority in your country).

We reply to requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email on your account.

10. How we protect it

  • Encryption on your phone (AES-256), with the key in the secure keychain.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our server.
  • Row-level security in the database, so each account sees only its own records.
  • Our admin tools are not public, and backups are encrypted.
  • Optional App Lock, and discreet notifications by default.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Young people

Bloom is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under the age at which you can agree to data use where you live (often 16), please use Bloom with a parent or guardian’s permission, and leave cloud backup off unless they agree. If we learn we hold cloud data from a child without permission, we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the version and date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply. Cloud backup will ask for your agreement again where the law requires.

Questions or requests: richsarbah11@gmail.com · Accra Resource Center LBG.

Bloom offers general information to help you understand your cycle. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and its fertile-window estimates must not be used to prevent pregnancy.
