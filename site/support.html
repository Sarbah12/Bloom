Moving to a new phone

Without an account: on your old phone, open Profile → Export my data and save the file (for example to Files or iCloud Drive). On the new phone, open Bloom and choose New phone? Restore a backup.

With cloud backup: sign in on the new phone under Profile → Account & backup and your records will sync.

Reminders aren’t arriving

Check Profile → Reminders, then make sure notifications for Bloom are allowed in your phone’s Settings. Reminders are discreet by default, so they read “A gentle reminder from Bloom”.

Predictions look wrong

Estimates improve as you log more periods. Check Profile → Cycle settings, and add any missing past periods in the calendar. If your periods are changing in your 40s, choose Perimenopause there. Predictions are estimates and Bloom is not a method of contraception.

Forgot your App Lock or passcode

App Lock uses your phone’s own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, so unlocking your phone’s passcode unlocks Bloom. We can’t reset it for you.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete all my data. This erases everything on your phone and, if you’re signed in, your cloud account. It can’t be undone. See the privacy policy for how long backups take to clear.

Health questions

Bloom gives general information and can’t give medical advice. If something worries you, talk to a doctor, nurse or pharmacist. If you have severe pain, very heavy bleeding, or feel faint, get urgent help.
